The architecture described in the image works as follows:
The architecture is composed of four required areas in which the different parts of the task are performed, and a main Electronic Control Unit working as the main computational unit of the system. 

V2X area:
The V2X area consists of a V2X module responsible for extravehicular communication with targets such as vehicles, infrastructure, cloud systems, and pedestrians. Through this module, the vehicle can share and receive navigation information, such as maps, and information about other street users, such as pedestrians [3] or vehicles [4].


Dynamics area:
The dynamics area is made of two kinds of components: Dynamics Sensors, designed to capture dynamics data from the vehicle, such as speed or steering angle [2]; and a Dynamics Electronic Control Unit that is responsible for controlling Dynamics Sensors and pre-elaborating and aggregating data to share with the main ECU.


Cockpit area:
The Cockpit area represents the interior sensing infrastructure, composed of cockpit sensors and its dedicated cockpit processing unit, which collectively monitor the psychological state of the driver by using cameras, as in the Driver Monitoring Systems (DMS) [5] proposed in the literature. 


Augmented Reality area:
The AR area comprises the main execution area, where the visual representations take place after being processed by the Electronic Control Unit, which executes body spatial transformations that generate the virtual frames [6]. AR output device, as the Picture Generation Unit [7] projects virtual light rays onto the windshield film, presenting the world-locked graphics, is responsible for the visualization of produced content for the Advanced Driver-Assistance Systems [2].



Main ECU area:
The main ECU is the component required to perform main computation and Augmented Reality content production. It communicates internally with all the other ECUs to receive input data from sensors and to send diagnostic signals to other areas, aggregates data and elaborates with AI models [1] the information to provide in output.
